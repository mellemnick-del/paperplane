Paper Plane Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

Paper Plane is a game made by Nick Mellem. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: nothing leaves your phone.

Information we collect

None. Paper Plane does not collect, store on a server, share, or sell any personal information. This version has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your best score on your iPhone so it is there the next time you play. It stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

Third parties

This version contains no third-party code, advertising networks, or analytics services. If a future version adds any, this policy will be updated before that version is released to describe what they collect and how you can control it.

Children

Paper Plane is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
