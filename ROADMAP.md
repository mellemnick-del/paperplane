# Paper Plane roadmap

## 1.0 (in App Review)
The game, how-to start screen, iPhone, iPad and foldable support.

## 1.1 (branch `release/1.1`, ready to build and test)
Google AdMob ads (rewarded "Smooth a crease" continue, paced interstitials), "Remove Ads"
purchase, Game Center leaderboard, rating prompt, pause when the app loses focus, 13+ rating.

## 1.2: reasons to come back (planned)
1. **Game Center achievements**: First flight (clear 1), Pencil pusher (25), Notebook ace (50),
   Spotless (15 with no crumple), Comeback (use "Smooth a crease").
2. **Daily challenge**: the same pencil layout for everyone each day (seeded by the date),
   with its own daily leaderboard.
3. **Share card**: after a new best, a notebook-style image ("I cleared 27 pencils") to share.

## Later
- Unlockable cosmetic planes and pencils earned at milestones (newspaper, graph paper,
  Zero Day green). Possible "style pack" purchase.
- Game feel: paper scraps on a crumple, near-miss graphite smudge, rising check-mark pitch on
  streaks, 120 Hz on ProMotion screens.
- Power-ups (tape roll patches a wing), occasional moving pencils.
- Sound and haptics on/off switch.
- Android version (decide after a month or two of iPhone numbers; Google Play needs a
  14-day closed test with about 12 testers for new accounts).
- Move ads to Teen + 13+ is done in 1.1; revisit EU distribution once there is a public
  business address (DSA trader rules).
