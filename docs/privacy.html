Paper Plane Privacy Policy

Effective with version 1.1

Paper Plane is a game made by Nick Mellem. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

The short version

Paper Plane itself does not collect any personal information. The game is free because it shows ads, and the ad service (Google AdMob) collects some device information to show and measure those ads. You can limit this, and buying "Remove Ads" turns ads off.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your best score, how many games you have played and whether you bought "Remove Ads" on your iPhone or iPad. This stays on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Paper Plane uses Google AdMob to show ads between some rounds and an optional video you can watch to keep flying. To show, limit and measure ads, AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), your approximate location based on your IP address, which ads were shown and tapped, and crash and performance data. Google's use of this information is described in its privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy and at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices:

  • When the game asks to "Allow tracking", you can say no. You will still see ads, but they will not be based on your activity in other apps.
  • If you live where the law requires it (such as the European Economic Area or the UK), the game asks for your consent before any personalized ads.
  • You can change tracking later in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Buying "Remove Ads" stops the between-round ads.

Purchases

"Remove Ads" is bought through Apple. Paper Plane never sees your payment details.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your best score is posted to the Paper Plane leaderboard through Apple's Game Center, under your Game Center name.

Children

Paper Plane is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
