How do I play?

Hold anywhere on the screen to climb and let go to dive. Fly through the gaps between the pencils. Each pair you clear is a point.

What happens when I hit a pencil?

A wing crumples. Your plane blinks for a moment while it recovers, and it wobbles a little more after each hit. Three crumples and the round is over.

I don't hear any sound.

Check that the ring/silent switch (or Action button) is not set to silent. Paper Plane respects silent mode and plays alongside your music.

Where is my best score saved?

On your iPhone only. Deleting the app resets it.
