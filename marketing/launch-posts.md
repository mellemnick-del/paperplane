# Paper Plane launch kit

Three vertical videos (1080 x 1920, 30 fps, with game sound), made straight from the game.
Each ends on a "Paper Plane: Notebook Flight / Free on the App Store" card.

| File | Length | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `clip1-watch-it-fly.mp4` | 15 s | Start screen demo, tap, a clean run to 7 |
| `clip2-crumpled.mp4` | 16 s | A few points, then three crumples and "Crumpled!" |
| `clip3-new-best.mp4` | 31 s | A long run that ends on "New best!" |

Tip: on TikTok and Reels, add a trending sound at low volume under the game sound, and put
the hook as on-screen text in the first second.

## Hooks (on-screen text for the first second)

- "I made a game where your notebook doodle tries to survive"
- "Hold to climb. Let go to dive. That's it. That's the game."
- "3 wings. Every pencil you clip crumples one."
- "Bet you can't beat 20"
- "POV: you're bored in class and your paper plane comes to life"

## Launch day checklist

1. Release the version in App Store Connect (Pending Developer Release > Release This Version).
2. Once it shows up in the App Store, copy the link (Share > Copy Link) into the captions above.
3. Post the launch post with clip 1, then clip 2 and clip 3 over the next few days.
4. Send the friends and family message.
5. Ask everyone to rate it in the first week.
